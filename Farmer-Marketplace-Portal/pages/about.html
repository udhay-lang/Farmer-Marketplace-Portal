Built to put farmers first

FarmConnect is a student-built marketplace concept exploring how technology can shorten the distance between the people who grow our food and the people who eat it.

Our story

Why we built FarmConnect

Across India, farmers routinely receive a fraction of the final retail price of their produce, while customers pay a premium for goods that have travelled through several middlemen and lost freshness along the way.

FarmConnect was designed as a final-year academic project to demonstrate how a direct, digital marketplace could close that gap — giving farmers a simple way to list and sell their harvest, and giving customers a transparent view of exactly where their food comes from.

What we stand for

Our core principles

Fair pricing

Farmers set their own prices with no hidden commission layers eating into their margins.

Radical transparency

Every listing shows the farmer's name, location and harvest date so customers know exactly what they're buying.

Community first

We prioritise features that genuinely help small and mid-sized farms reach new customers.
